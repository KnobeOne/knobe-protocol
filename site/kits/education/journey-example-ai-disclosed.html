What the journey says

Assignment 3 sequence

SOC 150 — Knowledge in the AI Era · Fall 2026 · Course Instructor

This sequence uses sealed KNOBE files. Each step is a plain-text file that records who made it, what it builds on, and the AI policy it was made under. Load this journey file into KNOBE Studio (knobe.org/studio) to start your step; upload the sealed file it gives you to the assignment.

Step 1: Knowledge Institutions in the Age of Context Loss

  • Sealed by: Instructor
  • Kind of work: Reading / source material
  • AI policy: AI policy not applicable

Step 2: Assignment 3: Reading Reflection with AI Disclosure

  • Sealed by: Instructor
  • Kind of work: Assignment spec
  • AI policy: AI allowed with disclosure. May use AI for terminology lookup, citation retrieval, or post-draft clarity review. May not use AI to draft or outline substantive content. All AI use must be disclosed specifically.

Step 3: Reading reflection

  • Sealed by: Student
  • Kind of work: Student writing
  • AI policy: AI allowed with disclosure
  • Builds on: Step 1, Step 2
  • Expectations: 300–500 words: summarize the reading, then respond to one claim in your own voice.
